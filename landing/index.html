Get TrustFirst

Two ways in, depending on your phone.

Android

Download and install the app. Your phone will ask you to allow installing from your browser — that is normal for apps that are not from the Play Store yet.

  1. Open TrustFirst in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen.
Download the app

iPhone & iPad

TrustFirst runs from Safari and installs to your home screen, so it opens like any other app.

  1. Open TrustFirst in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
Open in Safari
