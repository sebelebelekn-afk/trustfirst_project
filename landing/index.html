What you get

Three things it does properly.

Messages only you can read

Direct messages are end‑to‑end encrypted. They are scrambled on your phone and only unscrambled on theirs, so we cannot read them. There is nothing on our side to leak or hand over.

A wallet in Rands

Top up with a card, send a creator a gift, and they keep 80% of it. Card details are typed on the payment provider’s own page and never touch this app.

TrustClips

Short videos from people you follow, with the editor built in: trim, captions, music, and a cutout tool that runs on your own phone rather than on somebody’s server.

Going dark

Mute everything for a day or a month. The people who follow you are told you are taking space, so silence is not mistaken for being ignored.

Install

Get TrustFirst

Free, and no Play Store needed. Two ways in, depending on your phone. You can also just use it in a browser without installing anything.

Android

Download and install the app. Your phone will ask you to allow installing from your browser. That is normal for apps that are not from the Play Store yet.

  1. Open TrustFirst in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen.
Download the app

Android warns about anything installed from outside the Play Store. The file is built automatically from public source, so anyone can see exactly what is in it.

iPhone & iPad

TrustFirst runs from Safari and installs to your home screen, so it opens like any other app.

  1. Open TrustFirst in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
