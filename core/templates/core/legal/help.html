Help Centre

How things work, and what to do when they do not. If the answer is not here, write to us and it probably should be.

contacttrustfirst@gmail.com

Getting started

Making an account

You need an email address, a username and a date of birth. The date of birth decides which features are open to you and is not there to advertise at you — see the age limits.

Using more than one account

You can have several and switch between them without signing out each time. Saved accounts show a badge when one of them has something waiting, so you know before you switch.

The one thing you may not do is make a new account to get around a suspension.

Keeping your account safe

Two-step verification and passkeys are both in Settings. A passkey is the stronger option: it uses your phone's own fingerprint or face unlock and there is no password to steal.

If you think somebody else is in your account, change your password and write to us immediately.

Posting

Posts, clips and stories
  • A post can hold text and up to several photos or videos, which appear as one swipeable set.
  • A TrustClip is a short video for the clips feed, with an editor for trimming, splitting and adding stickers, text and sound.
  • A story disappears after 24 hours.
  • A poll lets people vote rather than reply.
Why my upload is taking a while

Photos and videos are shrunk on your device before they are sent, which takes a moment but means the upload itself is far smaller and far faster on a weak connection. You will see it shrinking, then uploading, with a percentage where one is available.

If it fails, tap the item to retry rather than starting the post again.

Drafts

A post or clip you start and do not finish is kept on your device so you can come back to it. Clearing your browser data clears drafts too.

Deleting something you posted

Use the menu on the post. It goes from TrustFirst, but anything somebody already reposted or saved is beyond our reach — the full explanation is in the Terms.

Messages

Why I cannot message somebody

Starting a conversation with someone new needs a verification badge. It is the only thing verification gates, and it exists because opening a message to a stranger is where most scams begin. Replying to a conversation already open needs nothing.

If they have blocked you, you will not be able to reach them at all.

This message was encrypted for another device

Not a fault. Your messages are encrypted for the specific device that will read them, and this one was sealed for a different device — usually because you have signed in somewhere new since it was sent.

Ask them to send it again and it will be sealed for the device you are on now.

Can I get old messages back

No, and this is worth understanding rather than discovering. Your messages are encrypted with keys that exist only on your device. Clear your browser data or lose the device and they cannot be opened again by anyone, including us. There is no backup to restore.

Disappearing messages

Tap the notice in the conversation, or open the chat settings, and choose: off, once seen, after 24 hours, or after 7 days. Either person can change it and both are told when it changes.

It removes messages from the conversation. It cannot remove them from a screenshot.

Going live

Starting a stream

You must be 16 or older. Check what is behind you before you start: whatever is in frame goes out immediately and there is no editing it afterwards.

Bringing people up

Viewers can ask to join. You see who they are before you accept, and accepting puts their camera and microphone in front of everyone watching. You can remove them at any time, and you are responsible for what they do while they are up.

Is my stream saved

The video is not recorded, so there is no replay. The chat and reactions are kept so somebody arriving late can follow along.

Coins, gifts and your wallet

How gifts work

You buy coins, and you spend coins to send someone a gift. The creator receives 80 percent of its value into their wallet and TrustFirst keeps 20 percent to run the platform. You must be 18.

A gift cannot be taken back once sent.

Coins and your wallet are different things

Coins are a virtual item. They have no cash value and cannot be turned back into money.

Your wallet is real money you have earned from gifts, and you can withdraw it.

Getting paid out

Minimum withdrawal is R50, and bank transfers take 1 to 3 business days. The bank account must be your own.

Tax on what you earn is yours to declare. We do not deduct it for you.

Refunds

Unspent coins can be refunded within 7 days of buying them. Spent coins cannot, because the value has already gone to a creator. If something went wrong at our end — a double charge, coins that never arrived — write to us and there is no time limit on us fixing it. Full detail in the Terms.

Verification

What the badges mean
  • Blue — a real person.
  • Silver — a real business.
  • Red — a government body or official.
  • Green — a child account with a verified guardian.
  • Gold — a public figure, granted by us.

A badge means we checked who somebody is. It is not us saying they are trustworthy.

What happens to my ID

It is deleted the moment your application is decided, either way, and automatically after 7 days if nobody has looked at it. Cover your ID number before photographing it — a reviewer only needs your face, your name and the document type.

Safety and control

Blocking somebody

Immediate, and it does not need us. A blocked person cannot message you, see your posts, mention you, tag you or interact with you anywhere. They are not told, and you do not owe anyone an explanation.

Reporting something

Every post, clip, comment, message and account has a report option in its menu. Reporting a message sends us that message, which is the only way we can act on it — we cannot read your conversations.

Anything involving a child, a threat, or somebody in danger goes to the front of the queue.

Going dark

Mutes every notification for a day, a week or a month, and tells your circle you are taking space. Notifications still arrive and still count up — you simply are not interrupted by them. You can come back early whenever you like.

Child accounts

Anyone under 18 needs an account linked to a verified parent or guardian. The guardian can see their activity, change what they are allowed to do, and close the account at any time. Nobody under 18 can buy coins or send gifts, with or without permission.

Eddie

What Eddie can and cannot do

Eddie is an AI assistant. It can answer questions, make images and read messages aloud. It cannot see your direct messages, it does not speak for us, and it is confidently wrong sometimes — check anything that matters.

There is a daily limit, which is what keeps it free.

Is what I type to Eddie private

It leaves TrustFirst. Eddie runs on an outside AI provider, so treat it as a conversation that goes elsewhere and do not paste passwords, ID numbers or somebody else's private information into it.
