Privacy Policy, Effective Date: 20 April 2026

TrustFirst ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is collected, used, and shared. By using TrustFirst, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. What This Privacy Policy Covers

This Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store and protect it, when we share it, and your rights and choices. We designed TrustFirst to be a safe global social platform for both adults and minors, with strong identity verification and safety systems.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Name, username, and profile information
  • Email address or phone number
  • Date of birth (for age verification)
  • Profile picture and uploaded content (posts, videos, images, messages)
  • Parent/guardian information for Kid Accounts
  • Content you create or send on the platform

2.2 Verification Information

  • A verification document you submit for a badge, reviewed by our team and then deleted. We ask you to cover your ID number first, and we keep only the outcome, never the document
  • Facial verification data (via camera-based verification system)
  • Device and authentication signals used for identity confirmation

This information is used strictly for verification and safety.

2.3 Device & Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system, and browser
  • IP address and network information
  • App usage activity (time spent, features used)
  • Interaction behavior (likes, follows, messages, views)
  • Crash logs and performance data

2.4 Content & Communication Data

  • Posts, comments, messages, and livestream activity
  • Shared media (photos, videos, audio)
  • Engagement data (likes, shares, bookmarks)
  • Message metadata (time sent, recipient, etc.)

2.5 Parent & Kid Account Data

  • Linked parent account username
  • Approval records from parents
  • Activity reports shared with guardians

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Provide the Service

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Enable posting, messaging, and social interaction
  • Support livestreaming and engagement features

3.2 Safety & Security

  • Detect and prevent fake accounts, bots, scams, and abuse
  • Enforce verification rules and restrictions
  • Monitor harmful or illegal content
  • Protect minors using parental controls

3.3 Personalisation

  • Recommend content and users you may like
  • Improve your experience on TrustFirst
  • Show relevant feeds and interactions

3.4 Platform Improvement

  • Fix bugs and improve performance
  • Develop new features
  • Understand user behavior in aggregate

4. How We Share Information

We do NOT sell your personal data.

4.1 With Other Users

  • Your profile, posts, and public activity are visible based on your settings
  • Verified users may interact with non-verified users under restricted conditions

4.2 With Service Providers

  • Google Cloud Vision (automated safety checks on images)
  • LiveKit (live video streaming)
  • Hosting and cloud providers (Supabase, Render)
  • Security and anti-fraud systems

These providers are only allowed to use data to support TrustFirst.

4.3 Legal Requirements

  • South African law
  • Court orders or legal requests
  • Law enforcement investigations

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • When you delete your account, all associated data is deleted immediately
  • Some limited data may be retained only where required by law or security obligations
  • After deletion, your profile and content are no longer visible on the platform

6. Children's Privacy (Kid Accounts)

TrustFirst takes child safety seriously.

  • Users under 18 must have a linked parent or guardian account
  • Parents must approve account creation
  • Parents may receive activity reports
  • Certain features are restricted for minors
  • We do not knowingly allow unsafe or unmonitored child usage

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you logged in, understand app performance, and improve user experience and security.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, row-level database security and fraud detection. Verification documents are held in private storage that only a reviewer can open, and are deleted as soon as the application is decided, or automatically after 7 days if it is never reviewed. However, no system is 100% secure.

9. International Use

TrustFirst is a global platform. Your data may be processed in different countries, including servers outside South Africa, to ensure proper operation of the Service.

10. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Edit your profile information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Control privacy settings

11. No Ads & No Data Selling

  • Does NOT show advertisements
  • Does NOT sell user data
  • Does NOT use personal data for third-party marketing

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are significant, users will be notified before they take effect.

13. Contact Us

TrustFirst Support Team, Location: Cape Town, South Africa.
